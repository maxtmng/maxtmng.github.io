It's so nice to meet you! I am a PhD candidate in Transportation Systems Analysis & Planning at Northwestern University, passionate about researching and modeling innovative and sustainable mobility solutions. My interest lies in public transit, network design, and data analytics.

I am also a chartered civil engineer in the United Kingdom with experience in large-scale project management. I represent the <a href='https://ice.org.uk/'>Institution of Civil Engineers</a> in the eleven Midwestern states in the United States.
